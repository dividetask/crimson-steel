# Glossary

Definitions of the terms used throughout the rules. Click any keyword in a chapter to read its definition here.

## Dice & Rolls

**Die Size**: The number of sides on every die.

**Roll**: A single throw of dice against a Target Number.

**Target Number**: The number a die must reach to count as a success.

**Base Target Number**: The Target Number a Check starts from before bonuses and penalties.

**Minimum Target Number**: The lowest a Target Number can be set to.

**Maximum Target Number**: The highest a Target Number can be set to.

**Critical Success**: A die that rolls the highest result, counting as two successes.

## Checks

**Check**: An action that can fail, resolved by one or more Rolls.

**Dice Cap**: The number of dice rolled for a Check.

**Minimum Dice**: The fewest dice a Check rolls.

**Maximum Dice**: The most dice a Check rolls.

**Prowess**: A measure of how practiced and capable a creature is.

**Attribute**: A creature's core score describing a raw capability.

**Attribute Check**: A test of an attribute for a task that training doesn't help with.

**Aptitude Check**: A test of a trained skill.

**Saving Throw**: A roll to resist or avoid an effect, or to recognize an illusion.

**Opposed Aptitude Check**: An aptitude check between creatures opposing each other.

**Single-Target Magic Check**: A spell or ability aimed at one creature: a casting roll against its Saving Throw.

**Multi-Target Magic Check**: A spell or ability aimed at several named creatures: one casting roll against each one's Saving Throw.

**Area Magic Check**: A spell or ability covering an area: one casting roll against each caught creature's Saving Throw.

**Combat Check**: An attack with a weapon: an attack roll against the target's defense.

**Magic Combat Check**: An attack with a spell or ability: a casting roll against the target's defense.

**Competency Bonus**: A bonus a creature adds to a Check it is skilled at.

## Bonuses & Penalties

**Bonus**: A modifier that lowers a Roll's Target Number.

**Penalty**: A modifier that raises a Roll's Target Number.

**Bonus Type**: The category a Bonus or Penalty belongs to: Circumstance, Guidance, Inherent, Morale, or Ascendancy. Same-type Bonuses and Penalties do not stack; only the strongest of each counts.

**Dice Modifier**: The surviving Bonuses minus the surviving Penalties after same-type stacking.

**Starting Value**: An adjustment carried into a Roll's score when the Dice Modifier would push its Target Number past the minimum or maximum.

**Starting Successes**: Successes added to a Roll's score before the dice are rolled — the positive side of a Starting Value.

## Outcomes

**Degree of Success**: The Supporting side's successes minus the Opposing side's.

**Degree of Failure**: The size of a negative Degree of Success.

**Check Outcome**: A Check's verdict: Success, Failure, or Fumble.

**Success**: A Check whose Degree of Success reaches the success threshold.

**Failure**: A Check that neither succeeds nor fumbles.

**Fumble**: A Check that fails badly enough to backfire.

## Damage

**Damage Type**: The kind of damage a source deals: physical, elemental, radiant, emotional, or force.

**Damage Severity**: How serious a wound is: Minor, Moderate, or Major.

**Minor Damage**: The least severe damage; heals fastest.

**Moderate Damage**: Mid-severity damage.

**Major Damage**: The most severe damage; heals slowest.

**Death Threshold**: The amount on a track — hit points, an attribute, or Magic Toxicity — that kills a creature once crossed.

**Damage Reduction**: An amount subtracted from each incoming hit.

**Damage Resilience**: A creature property added to a weapon's threshold when its damage is sorted into severities.

**Weapon Threshold**: A weapon value that sizes each severity band when its damage is sorted.

**Acid Counter**: The amount of acid on a creature; each turn it deals that much minor damage and then halves.

**Glory**: A weapon enchantment that treats its wielder as higher-Tier when attacking a higher-Tier foe, reducing the ascendancy damage reduction it suffers.

## Power & Tier

**Tier**: A measure of magical power and density. Tier 0 counts as one-half in formulas.

**Inherent**: A bonus that comes from a creature's Tier.

**Ascendancy**: A Tier-mismatch amplifier favoring the higher Tier — a bonus or penalty on a check, and damage reduction on a hit.

## Multiple Participants

**Supporting Roll**: A Roll on the side trying to make a Check succeed.

**Opposing Roll**: A Roll on the side trying to make a Check fail.

**Initiating Roll**: The first Supporting Roll — the creature attempting the action.

**Defending Roll**: The first Opposing Roll — the primary target.

**Spread Check**: A Check against everyone in an area, scored against each caught creature separately.
