# Common Glossary

Cross-domain vocabulary used by more than one domain. Definitions in this file take precedence over any per-domain glossary; per-domain glossaries should reference these terms rather than redefine them. When a term is introduced and used in only a single domain it stays in that domain's glossary; when it is used in two or more it moves here.

## Magic

**Magic Toxicity**: A Creature's accumulated exposure to magical effects.

**Toxicity Threshold**: The amount of Magic Toxicity that a Creature can have while still being able to benefit from magical healing and magical potions and oils.

## Power

**Tier**: The magical density of a creature, object, condition, or ability. Determines its relative power.

## Zones

**Zone Effect**: A persistent area on a Map with associated triggers — the Conditions half of a Zone (the Atlas side carries the geometry; see `atlas/atlas_glossary.md`). Models effects like Web's grappling area, Grease's slick patch, or Silence's noise-suppressed sphere.
