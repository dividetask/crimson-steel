# Common Glossary

Cross-domain vocabulary used by more than one domain. Definitions in this file take precedence over any per-domain glossary; per-domain glossaries should reference these terms rather than redefine them. When a term is introduced and used in only a single domain it stays in that domain's glossary; when it is used in two or more it moves here.

## Magic

**Magic Toxicity**: A Creature's accumulated exposure to magical effects.

**Toxicity Threshold**: The amount of Magic Toxicity that a Creature can have while still being able to benefit from magical healing and magical potions and oils.

**Tier**: The magical density of a creature, object, condition, or ability. Determines its relative power.

## Dice

**Target Number**: The number each die in a roll is compared against to determine it's effect. Abbreviated **TN**.

**Minimum Target Number**: Lowest possible TN.

**Maximum Target Number**: Highest possible TN.

**Bonus**: A positive modifier that decreases the Target Number of a Roll.

**Penalty**: A negative modifier that increases the Target Number of a Roll.

**Bonus/Penalty Type**: The label attached to each Bonus or Penalty identifying its stacking category. Bonuses or Penalties of the same type do not stack only the highest Bonus and Lowest Penalty (absolute value of each) is counted.

**TN Net Modifier**: The combined effect of all Bonuses and Penalties on a Roll, after per-Type stacking. When applying the Net Modifier would push the Target Number past the Minimum or Maximum, the overflow becomes a Starting Value.

## Rolls

**Roll**: A single roll of dice against a Target Number.

**Success**: A die result that meets or exceeds the Target Number.

**Failure**: A die that rolled a 1. Usually these results are subtracted from the number of Successes.

**Starting Value**: An amount added to the Degree of Individual Success before per-die contributions.

**Degree of Individual Success**: Net result of a single Roll. Sum of every die's contribution plus the Roll's Starting Value. Abbreviated **DoIS**.

**Neutral Result**: A die result between Failure and the Target Number. They do not contribute to the DoIS.

**Critical Success**: A die that rolled the highest result. Typically counts as two successes rather then one.

## Checks

**Check**: An action with a chance of failure. Each Check is made up of one or more Rolls.

**Supporting Roll**: A Roll on the side attempting the Check. Contributes positively to the Degree of Success.

**Opposing Roll**: A Roll on the side resisting the Check. Contributes negatively to the Degree of Success.

**Initiating Roll**: The Supporting Roll made by the creature attempting the Check. Always the first entry on the Supporting side.

**Defending Roll**: The Opposing Roll made by the creature targeted by the Check, when one exists. Always the first entry on the Opposing side.

**Degree of Success**: Net result of a Check. Sum of Supporting Rolls' DoIS minus sum of Opposing Rolls' DoIS.

**Degree of Failure**: Used when Degree of Success is negative; equals its absolute value.

## Effects

**Zone Effect**: A magical effect that occupies an area on a Map.
