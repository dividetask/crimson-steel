# Check Resolution — Glossary

Defines the vocabulary used by `check_resolution_design.md` and `check_resolution_tests.md`. Single-Roll terms live in the dice resolution glossary and are not redefined here.

## Checks

**Check**: A resolution made when a creature takes an action with a chance of failure. Composed of one or more Rolls, partitioned into a Supporting side and an Opposing side.

**Initiating Roll**: The Supporting Roll made by the creature attempting the Check. Always the first entry on the Supporting side.

**Defending Roll**: The Opposing Roll made by the creature targeted by the Check, when one exists. Always the first entry on the Opposing side.

**Supporting Roll**: A Roll on the side attempting the Check. Contributes positively to the Degree of Success.

**Opposing Roll**: A Roll on the side resisting the Check. Contributes negatively to the Degree of Success.

## Resolution

**Degree of Success**: Net result of a Check. Sum of Supporting Rolls' DoIS minus sum of Opposing Rolls' DoIS.

**Degree of Failure**: Used when Degree of Success is negative; equals its absolute value.
