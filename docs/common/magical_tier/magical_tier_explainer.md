# Magical Tier

This game takes place in a world saturated with magic. Everything has magic infusing its essence, and the power of Creatures, spells, and abilities can be categorized by how dense the magic inside it is. We call this density its **Tier** — a number from 0 to 6, with 6 being the density of gods. Most Creatures have a Tier of 0, but that is misleading, as even the lowest-Tier Creatures have some magical density; it would be more accurate to call them Tier 1/2. This density can be seen by any Creature of Tier 1 or higher, and appears as a hazy aura around them. With training, a Creature can learn to hide their aura, or even disguise it as a higher or lower one. Every time a Creature advances in Tier, all of their Attributes increase, as do their Hit Points and Mana. At higher Tiers, Creatures move faster, gaining multiple turns per round. As a general rule, fighting a Creature one Tier above you is dangerous, and a Creature two Tiers above you is fatal.

## Magic Toxicity

Receiving magical healing, potions, and oils temporarily infuses your body with magical energy — your **Magic Toxicity** — and that energy must clear before it reaches unsafe levels. The amount you can safely carry is your **Toxicity Threshold**, derived from your Charisma and your Tier. Once your Magic Toxicity rises above your Toxicity Threshold, magical healing, potions, and oils cease to work on you, and any magic that does push past the threshold poisons you, dealing Charisma Ability Damage. Magic Toxicity clears on its own over time, and there is no way to accelerate it.

## Attributes

Each Creature has six **Attributes** defining their physical and mental capabilities.

**Strength**: Raw physical power. Defines attack damage, carrying capacity, and your Strength saves to resist being crushed, restrained, or moved against your will.

**Dexterity**: Agility, reflexes, balance, and coordination. Defines attack accuracy with physical weapons and your Dexterity saves to avoid explosions and other area-of-effect damage. It also governs a number of Skills, such as picking locks, riding a horse, or sleight of hand.

**Constitution**: Physical fortitude and your Constitution saves to resist toxins and disease. Your Hit Points are based entirely on this Attribute and your Tier.

**Intelligence**: Mental ability to learn and reason. Increases the number of Skills you know, and governs your ability to notice illusions. Unlike most games, illusions cannot be disbelieved or seen through; instead, their inconsistencies can be detected using this Attribute and Class training. Your base Mana is based entirely on this Attribute and your Tier, though additional Mana is gained through leveling. All Creatures spend Mana on Class abilities.

**Wisdom**: Social awareness, common sense, and intuition. Defines your ability to notice things and get a sense for lies, and your Wisdom saves to resist compulsions. It also powers a Cleric's channeled abilities, and determines Initiative — the order in which Creatures act in combat.

**Charisma**: The strength of your personality and your ability to persuade or manipulate people. It also affects your ability to manipulate magical objects and channel innate magical abilities. Your Toxicity Threshold — how much Magic Toxicity your body can withstand — is set by this Attribute, as is how quickly that Magic Toxicity clears.
